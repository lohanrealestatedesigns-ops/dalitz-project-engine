# Dalitz Project Engine

Build a premium, high-authority contracting and engineering website for "Dalitzproject". The business focuses on high-end electrical, mechanical, low voltage systems, design, installation, and maintenance, and operates nationwide. 

CRITICAL DESIGN INSTRUCTION: Do NOT use cheap "local handyman" templates, basic tool icons, or generic layouts. The design must feel like a top-tier M&E (Mechanical & Electrical) engineering firm. Use aggressive, brutalist structural grids, heavy negative space, and sharp edges (use `rounded-none` or `rounded-sm`). Use React, Tailwind CSS, Shadcn UI, and Framer Motion for deliberate, professional reveal animations.

**Global Design System & Vibe:**

*   **Theme:** Technical, Precise, Nationwide Authority, and Industrial.

*   **Color Palette:** Use "Deep Industrial Navy" and "Gunmetal Gray" for primary backgrounds to convey serious engineering competence. Use "Safety Orange" strictly as a high-visibility accent color for Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons, active UI states, and thin structural borders. 

*   **Typography:** Use a highly structured, technical sans-serif (like Rajdhani, Roboto Mono, or Teko) for oversized, punchy headings. Use a clean, highly legible font (like Inter) for all body copy and service descriptions.

*   **Navigation:** A sleek, glassmorphism sticky header that turns solid Navy on scroll. Left: Logo text "DALITZPROJECT" in bold tracking. Center: Services, Projects, Nationwide Reach, About, Contact. Right: Solid Safety Orange button: "Request a Consultation".

**PAGE STRUCTURE & CONTENT:**

**1. Hero Section (Technical & Authoritative):**

*   Full-viewport height. Use a dark, heavy overlay on a placeholder background showing complex electrical panel wiring or a large-scale commercial build.

*   **Massive Headline:** "Precision Engineering. Nationwide Execution."

*   **Sub-headline (High-contrast gray):** "Expert design, installation, and maintenance across Electrical, Mechanical, and Low Voltage systems."

*   **Action Row:** "Book a Project" (Solid Orange Button) and "View Our Work" (Ghost button with white outline).

*   **Trust Bar (Bottom of Hero):** A thin, dark strip displaying: "Nationwide Deployment | M&E Specialists | 24/7 Support".

**2. Core Competencies (The Engineering Grid):**

*   A sharp, edge-to-edge bento grid with dark backgrounds and 1px steel borders.

*   **Card 1: Electrical Systems:** Focus on high-voltage, complex panel wiring, and infrastructure.

*   **Card 2: Mechanical & Design:** Focus on structural rough-ins, system layouts, and mechanical installations.

*   **Card 3: Low Voltage & Tech:** Focus on street lighting, smart systems, and low-voltage networks.

*   **Card 4: Nationwide Maintenance:** Focus on rapid deployment and lifecycle support for commercial and residential properties.

**3. Field Execution Gallery (Proof of Work):**

*   **Headline:** "Built to Spec. Built to Last."

*   A clean, asymmetrical masonry grid designed to showcase actual field photos. 

*   Create placeholders specifically for:

    *   Intricate distribution board wiring.

    *   Structural conduit laying during concrete casting.

    *   High-reach exterior lighting and street light installations.

    *   Completed modern multi-unit architectural builds.

**4. Lead Engineer Profile (Trust Section):**

*   A split-screen editorial layout.

*   **Left Side:** A placeholder for a professional, sharp headshot of the founder in a suit.

*   **Right Side:** "Led by Expertise." A brief bio highlighting the founder's hands-on experience, nationwide project management capabilities, and commitment to uncompromised safety and technical standards.

**5. Rapid Dispatch & Contact Footer:**

*   A massive, structured footer in Deep Industrial Navy. Top border is a 2px Safety Orange line.

*   **Headline:** "Ready to Deploy. Anywhere."

*   Include a sleek, minimalist contact form directly in the footer (Name, Project Type, Location, Message).

*   **Contact Info:** Provide placeholders for phone numbers, email, and a WhatsApp quick-link for rapid deployment requests.

Ensure the final output is highly polished, strictly professional, and bridges the gap between hands-on installation execution and high-level engineering design.
